# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, by email to security@estanza.casa. Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for them.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the impact you can see. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you share it anywhere else.

Only test against your own account and your own data. Testing against your own account on estanza.casa is fine; accessing other people's homes or accounts is not.

## Scope

This policy covers:

- the Estanza Card for Home Assistant (this repository),
- the Estanza web app at estanza.casa and app.estanza.casa,
- the Estanza API at api.estanza.casa.

Home Assistant, HACS and other third-party software are out of scope. Report problems in those to their own maintainers.

## What to expect

You will get a reply as soon as possible, normally within a few days. We will confirm whether we can reproduce the problem and tell you when it is fixed.
